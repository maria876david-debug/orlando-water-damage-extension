# Orlando AC Leaks: The Slow Drip That Damages More Than the Ceiling

In Orlando, the most common water damage does not come from a storm - it comes from the air conditioner. A blocked condensate drain lets water drip slowly, day after day, into the ceiling or the wall cavity, and because it is slow, nobody notices until the stain appears or the smell starts. By then the drywall is soaked, the insulation is wet, and mould has had weeks to grow in the dark. The fix is boring and cheap: check the drain line a few times a year, pour a cup of vinegar through it to clear the build-up, and make sure the overflow pan has a working float switch that shuts the unit off before it floods. A ceiling stain under an AC unit is never just a stain - it is a leak that has already been running for weeks.
