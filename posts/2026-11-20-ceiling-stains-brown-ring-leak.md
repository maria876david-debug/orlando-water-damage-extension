# Ceiling Stains in Orlando: What a Brown Ring Tells You About the Leak Above

A brown ring on the ceiling is not the damage - it is the leak's signature, and it tells you more than you might think. The ring forms at the edge of the wet patch, where water carrying minerals and dirt dries again and again, so a sharp dark ring usually means an old leak that wets and dries repeatedly, while a soft yellow patch that keeps growing means water is still coming in. Size matters too: the stain is almost always smaller than the wet area above it, because water spreads through the ceiling material before it shows. Press the spot gently - drywall that feels soft or crumbles is already damaged and will need cutting out, not just painting over. And never just paint a stain: find the source first, whether it is the AC unit in the attic, a bathroom seal upstairs or a roof valley. Paint hides the evidence while the leak keeps working.
