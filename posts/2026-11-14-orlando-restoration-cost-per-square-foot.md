# Water Damage Restoration Cost in Orlando: What the Per-Square-Foot Numbers Mean

Restoration quotes confuse people because two respected cost guides publish two different per-square-foot numbers for the same job. Both are on our cost page, and the difference is scope: Angi's figures are averages across completed repair projects, while HomeGuide's figures cover the extraction-and-drying stage specifically. A real invoice usually contains both stages.

By water category, Angi's average repair cost runs $3.50 per square foot for clean water, $5.25 for gray water and $7.50 for black water; HomeGuide's extraction-and-drying range runs $4–$6, $6–$9 and $9–$12 respectively. Across whole jobs, Angi's 2026 data puts the national average at $3,874, with most homeowners paying between $1,385 and $6,381. These are national planning figures, not quotes — your contractor prices your actual category, square footage and materials.
