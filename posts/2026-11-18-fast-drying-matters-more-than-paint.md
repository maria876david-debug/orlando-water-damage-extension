# Orlando Water Damage: Why Fast Drying Matters More Than New Paint

New paint over a damp wall is the most expensive mistake in water damage repair. In Orlando's humidity, a wall that looks dry on the surface can still hold moisture inside - and paint seals it in, where it feeds mould behind the surface you just fixed. Fast drying is what actually saves a home: water extraction in the first hours, then fans and dehumidifiers running until moisture readings are back to normal, not until the carpet feels dry to the touch. Only then does repair make sense - new drywall, new paint, new flooring on a wall that is genuinely dry. A restoration that skips the drying step is a repair you will pay for twice.
