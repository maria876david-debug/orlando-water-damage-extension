# The First Hours After a Leak or Flood in Orlando Decide the Whole Job

In most cities, a water leak is a problem. In Orlando, it is a race. Central Florida's sustained 70%+ relative humidity, shallow groundwater, and warm temperatures mean moisture trapped inside drywall or under flooring can start growing mold within 24 to 48 hours.

**Hour one: stop the source and make the call.** Shut off the water if the leak is plumbing. Then get help moving — connected local response teams in Orange County typically dispatch in an average of 45 to 60 minutes, and many offer a free on-site moisture assessment. Clean supply-line water can often be dried in place if it is extracted within 24 hours; wait, and the same spot becomes a removal job.

**What the first visit should look like.** Crews arrive with industrial extractors, infrared thermal imaging, and moisture meters to find every wet spot — including the water you cannot see behind baseboards and inside wall cavities. Bulk water comes out first, then antimicrobial treatment, then structural drying with air movers and commercial dehumidifiers.

**Days one to five: drying, monitored daily.** Standard structural dry-out takes 3 to 5 days of continuous dehumidification and air movement, with moisture readings checked against baseline dry standards. Severe flooding can extend that to about 7 days before rebuild work can safely start.

**Document everything from the start.** Florida policies generally cover sudden, accidental discharges like a burst supply line, while long-term seepage is frequently excluded — so photo documentation and moisture logs from day one protect your claim.
