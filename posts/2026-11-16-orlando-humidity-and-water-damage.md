# Orlando Humidity Hides Leaks: Where to Look Before the Damage Shows

Orlando's humidity does water damage a favour: it keeps things damp long after a leak stops, so small leaks hide for months. Check the places that stay wet quietly - under sinks, behind the washing machine, around the water heater, and the air conditioning condensate line, which clogs more often than most homeowners realise.

A musty smell in one room, a soft spot in the floor, paint that bubbles, or a water bill that creeps up for no clear reason are all early warnings. Catching a leak at this stage is a repair; catching it after the floor warps is a restoration job. A quick monthly look at those four spots costs ten minutes and can save thousands.
