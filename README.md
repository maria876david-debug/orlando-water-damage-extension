# Orlando Water Damage — Drying Estimator

Rough structural drying time estimate for Orlando homes.

A quick companion for the Orlando water damage restoration site. Get a rough structural drying time guide from the affected area, and reach the site for professional help. Estimates are a guide only, never a quote.

## Download

Installable packages are on the [Releases](https://github.com/maria876david-debug/orlando-water-damage-extension/releases) page — Microsoft Edge (.zip) and Firefox (.zip).

## Website

https://waterdamagerestorationorlando.us

## Privacy

No data is collected. The extension runs locally in your browser; no account, no tracking, no remote requests.
